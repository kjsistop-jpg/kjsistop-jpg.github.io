Introduction MIRAE SAFETY TECH operates Putting Mate, a mobile application designed to assist users with screen golf putting calculations. Putting Mate provides camera-based recognition of putting distance and elevation, green speed conversion, and visual putting alignment guidance. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with applicable privacy laws and Google Play policies. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when you use Putting Mate. ## 2. Camera access is used to provide the application's real-time text recognition functionality. Putting Mate does not require users to capture or manually save photos to use this feature. Camera image processing and any potential storage or transmission are subject to the actual functionality of the released application and the text recognition services it uses. ### 2.2 App Settings The application may store the following preferences locally on your device: - Selected game green speed - Preferred green speed - Camera zoom settings - Putting ball positions and aiming preferences These settings help improve usability and preserve your preferred configuration. ### 2.3 Advertising Information Putting Mate uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.
